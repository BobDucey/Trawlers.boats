What Makes a Trawler the Right Choice?

Trawlers use a displacement hull designed to move efficiently through the water at modest speeds, trading top-end speed for long range and fuel efficiency. That efficiency, combined with liveaboard-comfortable cabin layouts, is what makes trawlers the boat of choice for long-distance and extended cruising.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Trawler FAQ

What makes a trawler different from a regular cruising boat?

Trawlers use a displacement hull designed to move efficiently through the water at modest speeds, trading top-end speed for long range and fuel efficiency — that makes them well suited to long-distance and liveaboard cruising in a way that faster planing-hull cruisers aren't.

Single engine or twin engine trawler — which is better?

Single-engine trawlers are typically simpler, more fuel-efficient, and less expensive to maintain, while twin-engine models offer redundancy and easier docking — many long-range cruisers prefer a single engine specifically for its fuel efficiency over distance.

How much does a trawler cost?

Trawler prices vary widely by length and range capability — a smaller trawler can start in the low six figures, while a larger, long-range, liveaboard-ready model can run well into seven figures.
